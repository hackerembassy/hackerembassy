    Hacker Embassy

    About us

    We came from different countries and cities and opened a hackerspace in Yerevan.

    Our hackerspace is a gathering point for technology and creative enthusiasts. Whether you want to build projects, exchange insights, or just socialize, you will find a welcoming community of peers here.
    We believe in mutual support, equal voices, and collective decision-making, all aimed at fostering an environment where everyone can pursue their passions and grow together.

    We are not a business. We run on member donations, and we all help out by cleaning up, chipping in money, and getting new gear.
    If you are in Yerevan, love tech, and want to meet people like you, come visit!

    What do we have?

    • 3D printers (2 FDM and 1 SLA)
    • A laser all-in-one printer
    • Thermal printers for stickers and receipts
    • A few rework/soldering stations with hot air guns
    • Breadboards, radio components and parts
    • ESP32-s, Arduino-s and lots of modules for them
    • A set of hand and power tools
    • A fully equipped kitchen (sometimes with food)
    • Tea, coffee and snacks
    • Lots of poufs
    • Board games (from Munchkin to Monopoly)
    • VR headsets, consoles and a gaming PC
    • Coziness and good vibes
    • And much more
